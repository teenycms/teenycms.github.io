Teeny CMS · free and open source

A small website builder that keeps everything in plain files.

There's no database to set up. Upload Teeny to almost any web host, open your browser, and edit your pages by clicking on the text.

What you get

Everything a small site needs, and nothing it doesn't

Made for landing pages, portfolios, simple documentation, photo galleries and link-in-bio pages.

  • Edit on the page

    Click a heading and start typing. Add sections from the side panel, move them, and undo if you change your mind.

  • 32 kinds of sections

    Hero, gallery, pricing, FAQ, contact form, popups and more. Each one can sit left, right or in the middle, narrow or full width.

  • Plain files, no database

    Pages, settings and messages are saved as files on your own server. A backup is a copy of two folders.

  • Contact form and newsletter

    Messages land in an inbox in the Studio. Sign-ups download as a spreadsheet. Spam is blocked without CAPTCHAs.

  • Visitor numbers, no cookies

    See visits and popular pages without cookie banners and without storing IP addresses.

  • Owners and editors

    Owners look after everything. Editors write pages and manage images, and can't change settings by accident.

The editor

Edit your site right on the page

Click any text and type. Pick a section to see its settings, move it up or down, or make it narrow or full width. Visitors see your changes when you press Save.

Your look

Three themes to start from

Pick Minimal, Dark or Luxury, then change the colours, fonts, corners and width in Style. Choose a normal, centered, left or right layout for the whole site or a single page. Visitors can switch between light and dark.

Save your version as a new theme and share it as a .zip, or write one by hand. The theme guide shows how.

Safe by default

Security was part of the plan from day one

Teeny was reviewed for common attacks before its first public release, and every finding was fixed. Read how it protects your site.

  • Strong sign-in. Passwords are stored as Argon2id hashes, very common ones are refused, and sign-in can ask for a code from your phone.
  • Limits on guessing. Sign-in, codes, password resets and forms are rate-limited, so nobody can keep trying.
  • Clean content. Everything editors and visitors type is cleaned with strict rules, and outside scripts are blocked.
  • Plugins in a sealed box. Plugins can add new kinds of sections, but they can't run code on your server.

About two minutes

Install it in three steps

  1. 1

    Download

    Get the teeny-cms zip from the latest release. Pick the full zip, not the update.

  2. 2

    Upload and open

    Upload it to your web host, extract it, and open your-site.com/install.php in your browser.

  3. 3

    Save your rescue code

    Choose a look, create your account and keep the rescue code it shows. Then start editing.

You need PHP 8.1 or newer with the json and dom extensions, on Apache, LiteSpeed, IIS or nginx. Step-by-step help is in the installation guide.

Just want to look around first? Try it in your browser with GitHub Codespaces (free GitHub account), or run php -S localhost:8000 router.php in the Teeny folder on your computer.
